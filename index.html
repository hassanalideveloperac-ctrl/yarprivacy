Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

Welcome to YAR Plus Downloader ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your experience using our mobile application is safe and secure.

1. Information We Collect

YAR Plus Downloader is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII) of our users.

  • Local Storage Access: The app requires permission to access device storage solely for the purpose of downloading and saving media files (videos, audio) directly onto your local device storage at your explicit request.
  • Internet Access: The app requires internet connectivity to browse web pages and download media files from links provided by you.

2. How We Use Information

Since we do not collect personal information, there is no user data being harvested, tracked, or sold to third parties. All files downloaded through the app remain strictly on your local device.

3. Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to fetch web pages and download media contents.
  • WRITE/READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: Used to save and manage downloaded media files on your device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display download progress notifications.

4. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes open-source media libraries (such as AndroidX Media3, FFmpeg, and networking tools) to process and play media. These libraries operate locally on your device and do not transmit your personal data to external servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided information through our app, please contact us.
